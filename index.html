THE STORY

Alexandru Mihai Martinaş, known professionally as Kid Martinez, has always had a different view of life and a profound passion for music. Starting in the summer of 2022, inspired by the legendary MF DOOM, he dropped his first remix on SoundCloud.

From the early traction of "I Luv U" to the breakout success of "Freestyle Like Krazy", Kid Martinez has consistently pushed his boundaries.
